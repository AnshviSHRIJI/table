Growing a greener Maple Grove, one street at a time

GreenRoot is a volunteer-powered NGO planting trees, cutting waste, and teaching the next generation to look after the place they live.

Get Involved

Who We Are

GreenRoot started in 2019 as a handful of neighbors cleaning up a local creek bed. We're still neighbor-run today — no big office, no fundraising gala, just people who live here showing up for the land and for each other.

What We Do

Community Tree Planting

Monthly planting days that have put over 2,400 native trees into local parks and roadsides since we started.

Learn more

Neighborhood Compost Initiative

A weekly food-scrap drop-off that turns household waste into soil for community gardens.

Learn more

Youth Environmental Workshops

After-school sessions where kids ages 8–14 learn hands-on lessons about the ecosystems around them.
