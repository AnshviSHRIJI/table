Get Involved

We're a small NGO — that means every volunteer hour and every dollar is one we can actually feel. Here's how to pitch in.

Volunteer

  • Park clean-up & planting days — one Saturday morning a month, about 2 hours. No experience needed.
  • Compost drop-off support — Tuesday evenings, about 1 hour a week. Good for a regular standing commitment.
  • Youth workshop helpers — weekly during the school term, helping run sessions for ages 8–14.

Donate

Donations go directly toward seedlings and tools for planting days, compost bins for the drop-off points, and materials for the youth workshops — nothing goes to overhead we don't have.

We don't take payments through this site yet. Email us at hello@greenroot.org and we'll set up your gift directly.
