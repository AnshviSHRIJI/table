Our Programs

Everything we run grows out of the same idea: small, regular, local action adds up. None of these need special skills to join — just a willingness to turn up.

Community Tree Planting

We plant native trees and shrubs on public land that's been cleared, eroded, or just forgotten. Most sites are chosen with the city's parks team so the trees have a real chance to establish.

Who it's for: anyone — no gardening experience needed, we show you on the day.

How often: one Saturday morning a month, year-round.

Neighborhood Compost Initiative

Households bring food scraps to one of three drop-off points each week. We turn it into compost, which goes straight back into community garden plots around Maple Grove.

Who it's for: any household with a kitchen — no compost knowledge required to drop off.

How often: drop-off points are open every Tuesday evening; sorting volunteers help once a week.

Youth Environmental Workshops

After-school sessions for ages 8–14 covering recycling, water conservation, and the plants and animals local to Maple Grove. Each term ends with a small outdoor project the kids design themselves.

Who it's for: local kids aged 8–14, no sign-up fee.

How often: weekly during the school term.
